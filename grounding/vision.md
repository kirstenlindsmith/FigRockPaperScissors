# Vision

Rock Paper Scissors, fought by armies instead of hands.

Three armies of emoji soldiers hunt each other across an iPhone screen under Rock-Paper-Scissors rules: every soldier chases the nearest thing it beats and flees the nearest thing that beats it, and chases when the two pull equally. Contact converts the loser into the winner. Armies surge and collapse in waves until a single emoji has consumed the world.

The pleasure is in watching, not in playing. A battle is a short, unpredictable spectacle that resolves on its own and invites an immediate rematch. What the user commands is the setup — who fights, how many, and where they begin — and the speed of time while it unfolds.

It is for everyone from children to adults. A child must be able to open it and start a battle without being taught. It must stay legible and usable for people with limited vision, limited colour perception, or limited motor precision.
